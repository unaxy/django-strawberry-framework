## Approved timing and five-home contract

The maintainer explicitly approved the narrow sync exception with four conditions. A sync
flat leaf obtains its hook-only comparison context after the permission pass and before
ordinary field validation and consumer `Form.clean`. Denial calls no such hook. The early
result uses the same once-per-declaration/database cache that `.qs` reads. For that necessary
annotation leaf, `SyncMisuseError` precedes `FILTER_INVALID`.

Other sync flat leaves retain step-8 timing. Nested derivation remains step 3, including its
internal child calls; the existing async preawait and worker boundaries remain. On a multi-hop
flat path, only the actual annotation owner is prepared early; earlier independent hops wait
for application. Method-owned and already-owned comparison fields retain their ordinary path.

All five homes in [spec 027][spec] agree: Decision 8, Slice checklist, Edge cases, Test plan and
Definition of done. The source retains Django's normal single-pass field/`Form.clean` lifecycle.
It promises the needed cleaner before validation, rather than before pre-permission constructors.
